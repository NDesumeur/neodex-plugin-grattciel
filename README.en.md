# Gratte-ciel (Skyscraper)

A game of timing: stack the floors of a skyscraper. Each new floor slides from side to side above the tower; drop it at the right moment so it lands exactly on the one below. Build as high as you can, from the clouds up to the stars.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Controls

- **Click** or **Space**: drop the sliding floor (and start the game).
- **P**: pause.

## Rules

- Floors arrive alternately from the left and the right, faster and faster as the tower grows.
- The part of the floor that overhangs the one below is **cut off** and falls: the tower gets narrower and narrower.
- **Spot on** (within a hair): the floor keeps its full width, with a "Perfect!".
- **Three perfects in a row**: from the third on, each perfect **widens** the tower, up to its starting width.
- A floor dropped completely off the side falls, and the game is over.

The sky changes with height: the city, the clouds, then night and space.

## Statistics

The Records panel shows your **tallest tower** (in floors), the number of **towers** built, and the total **floors placed** and **perfects**.

## Data and sync

Your record and totals are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Gratte-ciel → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
